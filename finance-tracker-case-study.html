01 // Project Overview

Built for real users, not idealized sample data.

Many financial templates are either too manual to maintain or too complicated for everyday users. I designed two commercial spreadsheet applications that keep the customer-facing experience simple while a separate calculation layer manages the technical work.

The suite demonstrates end-to-end product development: requirements, workbook architecture, automation, dashboard design, documentation, compatibility testing, launch support, and revisions based on customer questions.

04 // Engineering Decisions

Complexity stays behind the interface.

The product needed to remain approachable without sacrificing data integrity, history, or long-term use. These decisions helped balance those requirements.

One source of truth

Challenge: Manual balance updates cause duplicate entry and inconsistent totals.

Solution: Dedicated payment and transaction logs drive balances and monthly summaries.

Status-aware reporting

Challenge: Paused or archived records should remain in history without affecting every metric.

Solution: Calculations include or exclude records according to their operational status.

Calendar edge cases

Challenge: More than one debt payment may be due on the same date.

Solution: Calendar staging logic supports multiple qualifying entries while excluding paused records.

Long-term scalability

Challenge: Customers should not rebuild formulas or validations as the workbook grows.

Solution: Input, formula, formatting, and validation ranges extend to approximately 5,000 rows.

05 // Quality Assurance

Testing beyond the happy path.

The QA process focused on the conditions most likely to cause customer confusion, incorrect totals, broken dashboards, or file-opening errors.

  • Blank and partially completed records
  • Multiple entries on the same date
  • Active, paused, completed, and archived statuses
  • Month and year selector changes
  • Dashboard and chart refresh behavior
  • Formula and worksheet reference integrity
  • Extended input and validation ranges
  • Excel file-opening and repair-warning checks
  • Google Sheets compatibility and help documentation
  • Customer-facing instructions and empty states
